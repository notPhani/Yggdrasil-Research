The demo story, under 3 minutes

  1. Replay January 2025: narratives form, and the R1 burst enters through BOT on 20 January.
  2. 27 January: the trigger finds two clusters, chips and power, with τ* = 08:00 UTC.
  3. The search: the best tree, rivals with odds, abstention, and the placebo p-value.
  4. Verdicts: H3 CONTRADICTED from the V3 report's own caveat; H4 UNRESOLVED; H6 SUPPORTED.
  5. "Same verdicts with every model switched off": the deterministic-only comparison.

Risks

  • Time: four evenings for six engines. Mitigation: a ruthless cut; you review instead of co-writing.
  • Download: start the moment the adapter exists, replay core first (anytime order).
  • SerpApi credits: 15 per case, cached; one case plus a few placebos.
  • Model weights: about 1–2 GB, with licenses checked before fetching (7.3).
  • Yahoo endpoint: archive on first fetch.
  • Disclosures: Claude for design and code; open models (GLiNER, NLI cross-encoder, MiniLM); prior work in Yggdrasil-Research.
