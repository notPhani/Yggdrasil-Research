Two external audits raised five "P0" findings and about twenty-five more. Each was either denied with a derivation or measurement, or accepted as a design change with a test that would catch the failure. Three disputed claims were settled by experiment before answering measured:

P0-4: quasi-Poisson and NB2 have different minimizers, so PIT is evaluated off-centreBoth estimating equations target the same mean and differ only in variance weighting, an \(O(n^{-1/2})\) efficiency difference (E3: 0.15–0.26%, about 5× below sampling noise). They diverge only if the mean model is wrong, and the PIT histogram is the detector for exactly that
P0-5: directed first-pop optimality is unprovenStill labelled derived, but E2 found no counterexample in 400 graphs and becomes a permanent differential test
Theorem 14.1 assumes a stable model existsThe precondition is stated, and the empty case has its own output. Under the locked template the empty case is impossible (Theorem 14.4, §7; E1)
Knobs overfit to 27 Januaryp0, λnode and γ are tuned on placebo days, which exclude case days by construction. θcand and j0 use referee titles, and the τ thresholds use the pre-period. The fair part, a design debugged on one event, is answered by the 2024 frozen-config benchmark
Metadata collisions merge different eventsNo merge rule uses metadata alone (rejected in Session 1 at 44% false merges). Step 1 needs the same title plus image, byline or names
Split or merge exactly at τ*Window steps are atomic, and a snapshot is emitted only after lineage operations finish. Conservation invariants run on every snapshot
k = 5–10 exhausts the budget\(3^8 \times 300\) nodes is about 2 million steps, sub-second
15-minute clocks can't order flash crashesCoarse clocks only remove evidence: anything in the batch containing τ* has first_seen ≥ τ*. They can never put an effect before its cause. The cost is that a cause in the last 15 minutes becomes "we do not know"
Publisher-timestamp bias (59% coverage)Admissibility never reads the publisher timestamp. first_seen is our ingestion clock, with 100% coverage
Yahoo deprecationThe replay runs from archived responses (D5), and the airgapped replay test proves it. Live mode has the google_finance fallback
SerpApi referee biasIt only labels English near-duplicate pairs, which is our scope. It is cross-checked against 50 hand-checked merges
Coordinated PR wavesA PR wave is real information; if it moved the stock, it is the right answer. Fabrication falls under A6
Placebo spoofingPlacebos are drawn from the past 90 days at case time, and first_seen is our clock, so no one can plant stories in the past. Planting decoys every day only raises the base rate, which is what placebos measure
Epistemic DoS via contradictory sourcesEqual-tier contradictions can force UNRESOLVED, the honest answer when equally credible sources disagree. They can never force a false SUPPORTED or CONTRADICTED, and never INCOHERENT (Theorem 14.4)
Stage-3 verification depends on SerpApiVerification runs on local models. SerpApi only retrieves pages, and the system must work without it (handover). The SerpApi-off replay test checks this
Slow-burn moves, crypto, non-US marketsOut of scope: abnormal equity events. The venue logic is already session-aware (Euronext). A multi-week trigger is a later extension
Hawkes edges are correlationAlready stated: edges mean timing, and count as influence only after F4, which includes a common-shock (hidden confounder) control
English-only blind spotAccepted as a measured limitation, not denied: the translingual benchmark measures the first-seen lag. Causes outside English fold into "we do not know" (A8 wording)
